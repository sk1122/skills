# priced-in

Answers "was it priced in?" with evidence. It pins the moment news first became public, searches news sites and X for earlier leaks and hints with the agent's own web search (reading posts through the read-only mdfromx.com mirror), pulls Polymarket price history and trades around that moment from the public APIs, explains each price move, and gives a verdict: priced in, partly priced in, or a surprise. It can also score a model's forecasts against the market (Brier score, calibration). Use `/priced-in` or ask whether a market knew beforehand.

See the [repository README](../../README.md) for installation.
